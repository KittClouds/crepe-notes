# Scanner A/B Test Scene

The sun beat down on the deck of the Thousand Sunny. **Monkey D. Luffy** sat on the figurehead, laughing as the wind caught his hat. Below him, **Franky** was showing off a new modification to the soldier dock system to **Usopp** and **Tony Tony Chopper**, who stared with starry eyes.

"Super!" **Franky** struck his signature pose.

Near the mast, **Roronoa Zoro** was napping, snoring loudly despite the chaos. **Sanji** kicked him awake. "Oi, moss-head! Dinner's almost ready. **Nami**-swan and **Nico Robin**-chwan are waiting!"

**Zoro** grunted, hand on his sword. "Shut up, dartboard-brow."

**Brook** laughed his skeletal laugh. "Yohohoho! Such a lively crew. It makes my heart race... though I don't have a heart!"

**Jinbe** steered the ship steadily, ignoring the bickering. He was more concerned with the two guests who had just boarded. **Shanks**, reddish hair blowing in the wind, stood by the railing talking to a stranger named **Harald**.

"I heard **Loki** is making moves in Elbaf," **Shanks** said quietly.

**Harald** nodded. "The Prince of Giants is not to be trifled with."

**Nami** looked up from her maps. "Did someone say Elbaf? **Usopp**, isn't that your dream destination?"

**Usopp's** knees shook. "I-I've suddenly come down with a can't-go-to-Elbaf disease!"
